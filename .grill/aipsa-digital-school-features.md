# Grill: AIPSA Digital School — Feature Requirements
Date: 2026-05-25

## Intent
Expand AIPSA ERP into "AIPSA Digital School" with a set of new features across attendance, auth, enrollment, fees, notifications, and the admin student view. The goal is a shippable foundation for each feature — some are ready to go live immediately, others wait on client decisions (SMS provider, voice call provider, LMS videos).

---

## Key Decisions

### 1. App Rename
- **Decision:** Rename from "AIPSA ERP" to "AIPSA Digital School" across the codebase and UI.
- **Reason:** Client directive.

### 2. SMS for Absence Notifications
- **Decision:** Build the SMS abstraction layer (service + hooks) now, but do not wire a live provider yet.
- **Reason:** DLT registration in India takes 3–7 business days; AIPSA has no provider yet. Recommended provider when ready: **MSG91** or **Fast2SMS**.
- **Alternative considered:** Wiring Twilio directly — rejected, not cost-effective for Indian carriers without DLT.

### 3. LMS Videos (Prospective School Demo)
- **Decision:** Parked — confirm with client before building.
- **Clarification surfaced:** Videos are a **sales demo tool for prospective schools** evaluating the ERP, not a B2C subscription product. Simpler architecture than originally assumed.

### 4. Password Pattern — First Login Flow
- **Decision:** Initial password = `global` + `aipsa` + `localId` + `class` + `studentId`. On first login, user is **forced to set their own password** before accessing anything. The pattern password is then invalidated.
- **Reason:** Operationally convenient for bulk account creation (no email/SMS needed); secure after first login.
- **Forgot password flow:** Admin resets the account back to the default pattern → user goes through forced-change on next login again. No self-serve recovery needed.
- **Alternative considered:** Randomly generated passwords with export sheet — rejected in favour of this simpler flow.

### 5. Student Joining — Two Paths
- **Decision:** Two distinct enrollment paths with different approval rules:
  1. **Admin-created account** → student is immediately active, no approval needed.
  2. **Self-registration with class code** → student is locked out until the class teacher approves.
- **Class code:** Class-specific, generated by the teacher. Admin can view all codes across all classes.
- **Self-registration fields:** Name, parent's phone number, date of birth only. Student ID and roll number are assigned by the school after teacher approval — never self-entered.
- **Reason:** Prevents duplicate/fake student IDs; keeps official data school-controlled.

### 6. Late Fee Automation
- **Decision:** Flat amount per installment, not a percentage or daily accumulation.
- **Configuration:** Each school admin sets their own grace period (days after due date before late fee applies) and flat late fee amount. Multi-tenant — each school owns this setting.
- **Reason:** Flat fees are transparent and cause fewer disputes with parents than compounding/percentage models.

### 7. Due Date Notifications
- **Decision:** Two reminders — **3 days before** and **1 day before** the due date.
- **Channel:** SMS (when provider is live) + voice call.

### 8. Automated Voice Call for Dues
- **Decision:** Simple **text-to-speech auto-dial** via **Exotel** or **Twilio Voice**. Reads out the due amount and due date dynamically. No conversational AI.
- **Origin:** Developer's idea, confirmed by client.
- **Status:** Build foundation, plug provider in when client is ready (same pattern as SMS).

### 9. Block Access if Dues Outstanding
- **Decision:** Any outstanding dues (even ₹1) blocks access to:
  - Report cards
  - Exam dates
  - Attendance view
- **Override:** School admin can lift the block per individual student (e.g., scholarships, fee waivers).
- **Hardcoded:** Not configurable per school — these three modules are always blocked.
- **Alternative considered:** Threshold-based blocking — rejected because it creates a loophole parents will exploit.

### 10. School Admin Student Details View
- **Decision:** Single student profile view for admin showing: marks, attendance report, fee dues, and miscellaneous activities.
- **Miscellaneous activities:** Disciplinary records, achievements, teacher remarks.
- **Who can add entries:** Any teacher assigned to that student's class + admin. Teachers cannot add entries for students in classes they don't teach.

---

## Surfaced Assumptions

- AIPSA assumed SMS could be turned on immediately — not possible without DLT registration (~7 days).
- The LMS "subscription" was actually a sales demo feature, not a B2C revenue stream.
- "AI automated call" was interpreted as conversational AI — clarified to mean simple text-to-speech auto-dial.
- Student self-registration was a hidden requirement not originally listed — surfaced through drilling the "teacher accepts students" requirement.

---

## Open Questions

- **LMS videos:** Confirm with client — is it a public-facing marketing page, or a demo account inside the app? Who creates the demo content?
- **SMS/Voice provider:** Client needs to procure MSG91/Exotel account and complete DLT registration before notifications can go live.
- **Notification channels:** Once SMS is live, should absence notifications also trigger a voice call, or SMS only?
- **Late fee cap:** Is there a maximum late fee per student per term, or does it apply to every missed installment indefinitely?

---

## Out of Scope
- Conversational AI calls (rejected in favour of simple text-to-speech)
- Percentage-based or daily-accumulating late fees
- Self-serve password recovery for students/parents (admin resets only)
- Configurable "which modules to block" per school (hardcoded: report cards, exam dates, attendance)

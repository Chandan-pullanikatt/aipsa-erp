AIPSA School ERP

Office / Support Staff — Getting Started

School ERP + Learning Management System

Non-teaching staff (office, librarian, warden, transport in-charge, accounts) are added by the admin and manage the operational modules. You are invited the same way a teacher is.

1. Get into your account

  1. Open the invite email and click the link to set your password (Accept Invite).
  2. No email? Ask the admin for the School Join Code and use "Join a School".

2. Modules you may handle

  1. Library: add/catalogue books, issue and return them, collect fines.
  2. Hostel / Warden: manage rooms, allotments, mess menu, gate-passes and complaints.
  3. Transport: maintain routes, stops and student assignments.
  4. Store: add items and record student purchases.
  5. Fees / Accounts: record payments, view due and defaulter reports.

3. Note

  1. You only see the modules your admin gives you access to.
  2. For anything you cannot see or edit, contact your School Administrator.

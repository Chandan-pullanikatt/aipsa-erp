EduBridge

Parent / Guardian — Getting Started

School ERP + Learning Management System

As a Parent you follow your child's attendance, results, fees and school life from one place.

1. Link to your child

  1. Use the School Join Code from the school with "Join a School", or the direct link they sent.
  2. Enter your child's admission number and portal PIN (given by the school) to link them.
  3. Set your password and log in to the Parent Dashboard. Multiple children show under one login.

2. What you can see

  1. Attendance: daily status and absence alerts.
  2. Exam Results: marks and the full report card.
  3. Fees: dues, payment history and reminders.
  4. Remarks & Activities: teacher notes about your child.

3. School life

  1. Events: photos and videos from school events your child took part in.
  2. Transport: your child's bus route, stops and timings.
  3. Hostel: room, mess menu, gate-passes (for boarders).
  4. Library: books currently issued. Purchases: items bought from the school store.

4. Notifications

  1. The bell icon shows alerts for attendance, results and fee reminders.

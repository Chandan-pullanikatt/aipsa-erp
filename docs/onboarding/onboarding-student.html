Student — Getting Started

School ERP + Learning Management System

As a Student you reach your lessons, homework, results and school life from your dashboard.

1. Join your class

  1. Open the Student Join page (student-join).
  2. Enter the Class Join Code from your teacher and your portal PIN.
  3. Set your password and log in to the Student Dashboard.

2. Learn

  1. LMS: open your subjects to read notes, follow links and watch video lessons. Your progress is saved.
  2. Homework: see assigned work, submit it online before the due date, and view your grade.
  3. Timetable: your weekly class schedule.

3. Track yourself

  1. Exam Results: your marks and report card.
  2. Attendance: your present/absent record.

4. School life

  1. Events: photos and videos from school events.
  2. Library: books you have issued. Transport: your bus route and timings.
  3. Hostel: your room and mess menu (boarders). Purchases: items bought from the store.

Teacher — Getting Started

School ERP + Learning Management System

As a Teacher you mark attendance, enter marks, set homework and share learning material for your assigned classes.

1. Get into your account

  1. Open the invite email from your school and click the link to set your password (Accept Invite).
  2. No email? Ask your admin for the School Join Code and use "Join a School".
  3. Log in — you land on the Teacher Dashboard.

2. Daily attendance

  1. Go to Attendance, pick your class/section and the date.
  2. Mark each student Present / Absent / Late and save. Parents are notified of absences.

3. Marks & report cards

  1. Go to Marks, choose the exam and your subject.
  2. Enter each student's marks and save. The admin compiles report cards from these.

4. Homework

  1. Go to Homework → Add. Choose the class, write the task and due date.
  2. Students submit online; open the homework to view and grade submissions.

5. Learning material (LMS)

  1. Go to LMS, pick your subject and add material — notes, useful links or video lessons.
  2. Students see it in their LMS and you can track their progress.

6. Other

  1. Timetable shows your weekly periods.
  2. Join Requests: approve students requesting to join your class (if enabled).
